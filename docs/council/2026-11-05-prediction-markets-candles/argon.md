### 3. Answers Q1–Q8

1. **Prediction markets:** Top 3 are **Candidates 1, 2, and 4** (table gives counterparties, edges, capacity, decay, and kill tests; drop sub-1c crypto cross-venue brackets, where 1 tick of slip erases 0.43c edge). Retail keeps paying because single-app convenience, small lot sizes, and clearing fragmentation deter institutional desks from policing thin pre-game spreads and bracket series (prior; check Novig/ProphetX pre-game lines too).
2. **Pair-locking and rebates:** Not reproducible. Buying Up at $p$ plus Down at $q$ ($p+q<1$) equals buying at $p$ and selling at $1-q$—**ordinary two-sided market making**, masked by International Polymarket's dual tokens and instant on-chain `mergePositions` capital recycling (prior). You lock <$1 only when *both* passive bids fill before price trends. Kalshi 15m pays 0 rebate with 0.6–0.9c/fill adverse selection even at 250ms cancels—fatal at ~1s. PolyUS hourly/daily brackets (~3% maker fill, no merge) lock capital 1–24h and rarely fill both sides cleanly. Cross-venue passive-passive dies from **adverse selection, legging risk, and tail queue position**: fast takers lift only your stale leg on spot moves while the other leg retreats, leaving naked losers.
3. **Longshot premium:** Viable in modified form (**Candidate 2**): ~$4k binary collateral + $3k futures margin ($350 max risk per expiry cluster $\implies$ $8–$25/d). Three corrections: (a) **VRP & skew**: options already embed VRP, and call skew lowers digital value ($-\partial C/\partial K$) below flat-vol GBM (prior)—explaining part of the student-club gap, though Portnaya's +5.6–11pp beats option digitals directly; (b) **Fat tails**: skip 96–98c NO (a 1.5pp jump-risk misestimate turns Kelly negative) and buy **75–90c NO (10–25c YES)**; (c) **Lockup**: skip multi-month holds; quote **1–14d strikes** and exit into Portnaya's **4-hour half-life**. With offshore options unavailable, use Deribit's free public API (prior) as pricing oracle and delta-hedge net BTC/ETH exposure via US-regulated **CME Micro futures/options (MBT/MET) or Coinbase CDE nano-perps** (prior), exiting near-strike positions >24h pre-expiry to avoid pin-gamma whipsaw (prior).
