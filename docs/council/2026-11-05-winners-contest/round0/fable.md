# A. Can we reverse-engineer the winners?

Partially. Public per-wallet data recovers **mechanism**, not **signal**.

**Recoverable:** edges whose fingerprint is in trade placement itself — maker-rebate market making (two-sided fills, maker share, rebate-dominated P&L decomposition, like gabagool22); resolution sniping (buys at 95–99¢ concentrated in the final minutes before resolution); negative-risk/combo arbitrage (SPLIT/MERGE/CONVERSION paired with multi-outcome trades where the basket sums past $1); longshot-bias harvesting (persistent NO-buying in high-price buckets with calibration better than price **(prior)**); latency taking (reaction time to external price ticks or news events, if we timestamp the external event); liquidity-reward farming (reward income dominating, near-flat inventory).

**Not recoverable:** (1) the *content* of private models — a sports or elections forecaster's trades show category, timing, and calibration but not why; we can verify the edge exists, not copy it. (2) Off-platform-hedged books: a wallet buying crypto-threshold YES hedged on a perp exchange **(prior)** shows platform P&L that is only half a basis trade — its on-platform leg can even look like a steady loser. (3) Informed/insider flow in mentions/awards markets — indistinguishable from luck at small n. (4) Skill vs. luck itself: a top-250-by-profit list is a max order statistic over ~hundreds of thousands of wallets; some leaders are pure variance.

**Traps for naive analysis:** survivorship/selection on outcomes (ranking by realized profit guarantees lucky coin-flippers at the top; antidote: condition on *process* features — calibration by entry price, markouts — not outcomes); rebates and rewards inside P&L (a "winner" may be gross-negative on price prediction and net-positive on the fee schedule — the brief's bot cohort, +$190k on $328M, is exactly this); off-platform hedges (above); account splitting (inflates "independent wallet" breadth and defeats per-wallet significance tests); copy-trading (N wallets echo one signal with lag — they're one effective observation); wash/airdrop/sybil farming (self-trades transfer P&L between own wallets, manufacturing both fake winners and fake losers).

# B. Pre-registered edge hypotheses

1. **Crypto up/down rebate MM.** Mechanism: quote both sides of 5/15-min markets; takers pay fees, makers collect spread + rebates **(prior + brief)**. Signature: both-outcomes share >60%, maker share >70%, rebate income >30% of net P&L, >500 trades/day in crypto up/down. Prevalence: 20–40 of top 250; few in consistent cohort at modest size. Magnitude: ~5–20 bps/trade net, >90% positive weeks. Replication: yes on Kalshi crypto markets if maker queue priority attainable at 1 s. Falsify: net P&L minus rebates ≤ 0 CI excludes positive… edge is rebates, reclassify.
2. **Resolution sniping / certainty convergence.** Mechanism: buy 96–99.5¢ outcomes already decided in reality but not yet resolved; sellers pay for capital velocity **(prior)**. Signature: >70% of buys at price ≥0.95 within 24h of resolution, hold-to-resolution, win rate >97%. Prevalence: 15–30 of top 250; heavily represented in consistent cohort. Magnitude: 1–4% per trade, >85% positive weeks. Replication: yes, both platforms, trivially at 1 s. Falsify: bucket-level win rate at 98¢ ≤ 98% (no edge over price).
3. **Negative-risk / combo arbitrage.** Mechanism: multi-outcome books where YES prices sum >$1 or <$1; buy/sell the set, MERGE/SPLIT **(prior)**. Signature: CONVERSION/SPLIT/MERGE events within minutes of trades spanning all outcomes of one event; near-zero directional exposure. Prevalence: 5–15 wallets. Magnitude: riskless 0.5–3% per set, ~100% positive weeks, capacity-limited. Replication: Kalshi multi-leg markets, yes. Falsify: basket executions don't sum past $1 after fees.
4. **Binance-lead stale-quote sniping.** Mechanism: take stale crypto-threshold quotes within ~1 s of spot moves; slow makers pay **(prior)**. Signature: taker share >90%, trade timestamps within 2 s after large external BTC/ETH tick moves, short holds via round-trip. Prevalence: 5–15. Magnitude: thin per-trade, high Sharpe. Replication: brief says Kalshi 15-min is taker-efficient at retail latency — marginal; Polymarket US unknown. Falsify: markout at +60 s ≤ 0.
5. **Sports in-play latency (courtsiding).** Mechanism: faster event feed than the book; slow in-play quoters pay **(prior)**. Signature: in-play sports trades clustered seconds after scoring events, strongly positive +1-min markouts, taker-heavy. Prevalence: 5–10. Magnitude: 2–10% per trade bursts. Replication: Kalshi sports, yes if feed bought. Falsify: markouts flat relative to event timestamps.
6. **Pre-game closing-line value.** Mechanism: bet when Polymarket lags sharp sportsbook consensus (Pinnacle) **(prior)**. Signature: pre-game sports entries whose prices sit ≥2¢ off contemporaneous sharp lines, hold-to-resolution, win rate ~55–58% at even prices. Prevalence: 10–25. Magnitude: 2–5% ROI/trade, 60–70% positive weeks. Replication: yes on Kalshi sports. Falsify: entry prices show no systematic gap vs. sharp reference lines.
7. **Longshot-bias tail selling.** Mechanism: retail overpays for lottery YES; sell it (buy NO at 85–97¢) across politics/novelty markets **(prior)**. Signature: NO-side concentration, entry bucket 0.85–0.97, many markets, hold-to-resolution, calibration above price. Prevalence: 20–40, strongly in consistent cohort. Magnitude: 1–5%/trade, >80% positive weeks with rare large losses. Replication: yes, both platforms. Falsify: bucket calibration equals price (no excess).
8. **UMA/resolution-rules lawyering.** Mechanism: read resolution criteria more carefully than the crowd; buy mispriced markets where the *rule*, not the event, decides **(prior)**. Signature: concentration in markets with disputed/ambiguous resolutions, entries mid-price, large win rate on resolution. Prevalence: 3–8. Magnitude: 10–30%/trade, lumpy weeks. Replication: yes (human-in-loop). Falsify: no excess win rate in disputed-resolution markets.
9. **News-parse politics/mentions sniping.** Mechanism: automated parsing of transcripts/tweets beats manual traders by minutes **(prior)**. Signature: mentions-market buys within 60 s of the triggering utterance timestamp, taker, near-certain resolution. Prevalence: 5–10. Magnitude: 5–20%/trade. Replication: yes. Falsify: trade times not clustered post-event.
10. **Weather model edge.** Mechanism: free NWS/ECMWF ensembles beat retail priors in weather markets **(prior)**. Signature: weather-category concentration, entries 12–48h pre-resolution, calibration above price. Prevalence: 3–8. Magnitude: 3–8%/trade, >75% positive weeks. Replication: Kalshi weather, yes. Falsify: calibration ≤ price.
11. **Whale-fade / flow toxicity sorting.** Mechanism: provide liquidity selectively against known-dumb counterparties, pull quotes against sharp ones **(prior)**. Signature: maker fills whose counterparty wallets have negative lifetime markouts. Prevalence: 5–10. Needs counterparty join. Falsify: counterparty-conditional markouts indistinguishable.
12. **Long-tail wide-spread MM.** Mechanism: quote illiquid markets at wide spreads; impatient retail crosses **(prior)**. Signature: maker share >60% in markets below median volume, inventory mean-reverting, spread capture positive. Prevalence: 10–20. Magnitude: 2–6% effective spread. Replication: yes. Falsify: adverse selection (negative markouts) eats spread.
13. **Capital-velocity exit liquidity.** Mechanism: the sellers in #2 — sell 97¢ positions early, redeploy; it's rational, and the *pair* proves #2's persistence. Signature: round-trip sells at 0.9–0.99 before resolution with high turnover and positive net P&L. Prevalence: 5–10. Falsify: turnover-adjusted ROI below hold-to-resolution counterfactual.
14. **Cross-platform Kalshi↔Polymarket arb.** Mechanism: same event priced differently; buy cheap leg each side **(prior)**. Signature: one-sided Polymarket exposure in markets with a Kalshi twin, entries when gap >3¢ (needs Kalshi price join). Prevalence: 5–12. Replication: is the edge. Falsify: no gap at entry times.
15. **Multi-account rebate/reward splitter.** Mechanism: one operator splits #1/#2 flow across wallets to stay under reward caps and radar — the owner's thesis. Signature: see C. Prevalence: 2–5 entities, 10–60 wallets. Falsify: cluster wallets' P&L uncorrelated and funders disjoint.
16. **Airdrop/points speculation farming (anti-edge).** Mechanism: volume farmed for expected future tokens; P&L noise **(prior)**. Signature: high volume, near-zero net P&L, self-crossing patterns. Listed to *exclude* from edges. Falsify: n/a — control group.

# C. Detecting the hidden entity

Join two evidence families. **On-chain:** shared funder/first-deposit source, shared withdrawal sinks, gas topped up from one address, sequential wallet creation times. **Behavioral:** co-trading (same market, same side within 1 s) far above chance; near-identical size-bucket histograms and time-of-day activity vectors; identical quoting parameters (tick offsets, order sizes, cancel cadence); correlated weekly P&L; staggered activation (new wallet spins up when another nears a size/visibility threshold). Most likely edge class: capacity-constrained, calibration-flavored flow — resolution sniping (#2) or rebate MM (#1) — because those scale by adding accounts, not size, matching "small, calibrated, under the radar."

False positives, and separators: **copy-traders** follow a leader with consistent positive lag (leader–follower asymmetry; an entity's wallets fire simultaneously); **shared vendor bots** behave identically but have disjoint funding graphs and uncorrelated deposit/withdrawal timing; **sybil airdrop farmers** cluster on-chain but show volume without calibrated profit; **declared MM firms** cluster both ways but are large, not small. Require ≥2 independent evidence types *plus* positive holdout P&L, per scoring rules.

# D. Data requests

1. **maker_flag** per fill: boolean, from CLOB fill side (resting order = maker); if unavailable, proxy = fill price on the passive side of prevailing quote.
2. **best_bid/best_ask at fill time** per trade, and **markout_{60s, 1h, resolution}** = (mark − fill price) × side, mark = mid at horizon or settlement value.
3. **external_ref**: Binance 1 s mid for BTC/ETH at fill time (for crypto markets); sharp-book closing line per sports market; event timestamps for mentions markets.
4. **time_to_resolution_s** and **time_since_market_open_s** per trade.
5. **counterparty_wallet** per fill (from on-chain order matching) + counterparty lifetime markout.
6. **Cluster graph**: per wallet — funder address, first funding tx source and time, withdrawal destinations, gas-funding source; pairwise co-trade rate (same market/side, Δt ≤ 1 s, vs. permutation-null), size-histogram cosine similarity, hour-of-day vector correlation.
7. **Net directional exposure per underlying** (e.g., all BTC markets combined, signed, hourly) — detects internal hedging/wash and off-platform-hedge candidates.
8. **neg_risk_flag**: SPLIT/MERGE/CONVERSION within 10 min of trades covering ≥2 outcomes of one event; basket cost at execution.
9. **wallet_week_pnl** table (net of fees, with and without rebates/rewards) — for the ≥80%-positive-weeks test, decomposed so rebate-only winners are separable.
10. **Calibration table** per wallet: entry-price bucket (2¢ bins) × resolved outcome, hold-to-resolution trades only, with Brier score vs. price-implied baseline.
